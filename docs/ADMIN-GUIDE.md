# Admin guide

Sign in at **/admin/login**. What you see in the sidebar depends on your role (Admin › Roles & permissions).

## Replace photos and videos (most important first step)

1. **Media library** → filter **Placeholders only** to see every empty slot.
2. Open an item → **Replace file** (JPG, PNG, WEBP, AVIF, SVG / MP4, WebM, MOV). The new file appears everywhere that slot is used.
3. Set **Alt text** in English and Arabic, and click the image to set the **focal point** used when cropping.
4. For videos, choose a **Poster image** and autoplay/muted/loop/controls. Large videos: paste a CDN URL in *External / CDN URL*.

## Brand & design

**Theme builder** → pick a theme on the left (or **Duplicate** a preset), adjust controls, switch Desktop / Tablet / Mobile, then **Save draft** → **Preview site** → **Publish theme**. Upload the final logo under *Brand*.

## Homepage

**Homepage** → toggle sections on/off, reorder with ▲▼, **Edit** to change text (EN + AR), button, link, desktop/mobile image and video. Announcement bar text and the footer text are on the right.

## Menus

**Navigation** → reorder items, edit labels and links. Mega-menu columns take one link per line: `English | العربية | /link`.

## Products & pricing

- **Metal rates** — enter the new QAR/gram rate; all product prices update instantly. History is kept.
- **Products** — the live price preview at the top of the editor shows the full breakdown as you edit weights and charges. Use **Import CSV** to bring products over from the current website.
- **Pricing rules** — tax %, delivery fee, free-delivery threshold, price-lock minutes.

## Showrooms & stock

Replace *Branch 01–03* with real showrooms (address, zone, phone, WhatsApp, map link, hours, photos). **Inventory** → ± to adjust, ⇄ to transfer between showrooms, ✕ to mark damaged.

## Orders

Open an order to see items, the rate at the time of purchase, delivery details and history. Update status (customers are notified) or **Refund & restock**.

## Languages

**Languages** lets you edit any interface text in English or Arabic; edited values are highlighted in gold.
